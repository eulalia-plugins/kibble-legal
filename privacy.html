Kibble Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kibble may collect, why it is used, how it is stored, and what choices users have regarding that information.

1. Information Kibble Collects

Kibble may store information needed to provide its features, including Discord user IDs, Discord server IDs, Discord channel or message IDs when required for temporary game or moderation state, treat balances, inventory and consumable quantities, DAWG TUOYS™️ collections, marketplace listings, trading activity, game statistics, gambling statistics involving Kibble’s virtual currency, achievements, XP, levels, pup classes, quests, daily streaks, cosmetics, lottery entries, cooldowns, moderation-related bot state, muzzle and punishment progress, and other statistics necessary for Kibble’s features.

Kibble does not require or store your Discord password.

2. Message Content

Kibble may process Discord message content when necessary to recognize commands, run games and economy features, apply moderation features, and apply temporary speech transformations. Kibble is not designed to permanently archive normal conversation content simply because a user speaks in a channel.

3. Webhooks

Some Kibble features may use Discord webhooks to repost transformed messages while preserving a user’s display name and avatar.

4. Why Information Is Collected

Information is used to operate Kibble and provide persistent balances, inventories, collectibles, trading, marketplace listings, games, leaderboards, achievements, levels, classes, cosmetics, quests, daily streaks, statistics, moderation tools, cooldowns, server events, and other Kibble functionality.

5. Data Storage

Kibble stores persistent gameplay information in a database used by the bot. Reasonable efforts may be made to protect this data, but no system can guarantee perfect security or permanent availability.

6. Data Sharing

Kibble does not sell personal information and does not intentionally provide stored user data to advertisers. Information may be processed by services necessary to operate the bot, such as Discord and the infrastructure used to host Kibble.

7. Discord

Kibble operates on Discord and therefore depends on information Discord makes available to bots through its API. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s own privacy policy and terms.

8. Server Administrators and Moderators

Kibble includes features that may allow authorized server staff to perform actions involving other users. Kibble relies on Discord permissions to restrict certain administrative features.

9. Data Retention

Gameplay data may remain stored for as long as necessary to maintain Kibble’s persistent features. Some temporary information is automatically removed after it is no longer needed.

10. Server Resets

Authorized administrators may use Kibble’s server-reset functionality to remove Kibble data associated with that Discord server. Once reset, this data may not be recoverable.

11. Removing Kibble From a Server

Removing Kibble from a Discord server does not necessarily guarantee that all previously stored database records are immediately deleted.

12. User Data Requests

Users may contact the Kibble owner to ask about data associated with their Discord account. Where reasonably possible, users may request deletion of Kibble data associated with their Discord user ID.

13. Children’s Privacy

Kibble is intended to be used only by individuals who are permitted to use Discord under Discord’s applicable age requirements.

14. Security

Users should never send Kibble Discord passwords, authentication tokens, banking information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information.

15. Automated Processing

Many Kibble features operate automatically, including reward calculations, statistics, message transformations, random game outcomes, cooldowns, achievements, quests, and inventory updates.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Kibble changes. Continued use of Kibble after an updated policy is published means you acknowledge the revised policy.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, deletion requests, or concerns about Kibble’s handling of information, contact the Kibble bot owner through the official Discord server or other contact method provided with the bot.

Kibble collects only the information reasonably necessary to operate its features and does not sell user data.
